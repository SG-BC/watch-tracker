v1.6.3
- MDBList to Kodi now also fills in episode ratings, for episodes in your Kodi library that have no rating yet. A rating never marks an episode watched. Season ratings are still left out.
- Fix: the MDBList part of a sync to Kodi, WeTrakr or AniList was described as if it went to Simkl ("filed on Simkl", "Simkl has none"). Those lines now name the real destination; the "filed on Simkl" line only appears for Simkl.
- Every sync and preview, for every From/To pair, now writes its on-screen summary to the debug log (one line, "<From> to <To> summary | ..."). Before, syncs from WeTrakr/AniList and MDBList to Kodi/WeTrakr/AniList logged no counts.
- Syncing to an empty Kodi library now says so up front: syncing to Kodi only updates movies and episodes that are already in the library.

v1.6.2
- Fix: Edit recent items > Mark as Watched > "This episode and all previous episodes" only marked the one episode when the show was not in the Kodi library (e.g. played from Plex). The episode list now comes from the Kodi library, else Simkl's catalog (no Simkl login needed when the Simkl id is known), else WeTrakr (when logged in). Specials and episodes not aired yet are left out. With no list at all, earlier seasons are sent whole (each service fills them in) plus episodes 1 to N of this season.
- The confirmation now always says what will be marked, e.g. "5 episodes (S01E01-S02E03)" or "Season 1 (whole) + S02E01-E03 (no episode list found)".
- Marking watched by hand now always counts as a new watch, also for episodes already watched: every service gets it, and Kodi raises the play count by one.
- Recent items keep the services' own ids (the Simkl id from the scrobble answer, the WeTrakr id once looked up), so the editor needs no lookup.

v1.6.1
- Fix: syncing FROM WeTrakr was very slow (about 100 calls) and stopped with an error on the ratings ("float() argument must be ... not 'dict'"). It now uses WeTrakr's compact lists (up to 5,000 rows a call, walked with the cursor WeTrakr returns): a whole library is a handful of calls. Rows carry ids only, so a WeTrakr-to-AniList match relies on AniList/MAL ids when WeTrakr has them. The first rows of each list are written to the debug log to confirm the field names.

v1.6.0
- New service: AniList (anime). Log in by pasting the code AniList shows (it has no PIN-code flow); tokens last a year. A watch sets your progress (first season only, matched by AniList/MAL id or an exact title and year), ratings use your AniList scoring system automatically. Off until you log in.
- Syncing: WeTrakr and AniList are now in the From / To lists. Kodi, Simkl and MDBList can write to both; WeTrakr can be read into Kodi, Simkl, MDBList and AniList; AniList into Kodi, Simkl and WeTrakr. Add-only. AniList is paced (about one item every 2 seconds).
- New: "Edit recent items..." (General). The last 5 movies, shows, seasons and episodes you played: mark watched (now, or a date and time you pick; for episodes this one only or everything up to it), mark unwatched (rating removed too; a show or season clears all its episodes), rate (movies and shows; episodes if episode ratings are on) or remove the rating. Sent to every service you are logged in to and, by default, the Kodi library.
- New: watchlist widget. Add Watch Tracker as a video add-on widget for "Your Next Movies" / "Your Next Shows". Sources: Simkl, MDBList and WeTrakr watchlists (each can be switched off); the list is refreshed in the background and read from disk by the widget. Titles in your Kodi library play; the others show their poster only.
- Ratings: "Date ratings with the watch date" is back (on by default); manual ratings follow it too.

v1.5.0
- New service: WeTrakr. PIN-code login, scrobbling (own "mark as watched at" value), episode and movie ratings, mark-watched fallback. Off until you log in. Not yet in the From/To sync lists.
- Fix: the connection status row ("Connected as @username" / "Disconnected") was missing: Kodi rejected its control (format="string" is not valid on a button) and dropped the row. It is now a read-only text field.
- Fix: a show found by title alone could be the wrong one (Naked Attraction (Spain) matched the 2016 original). The first episode of season 1 now has to air in the show's premiere year (+-1), and the folder name (e.g. "Naked Attraction Spain") is tried before Kodi's show title. Applies to MDBList and Simkl searches.
- Settings: "Ignore folders..." and "Ignore video add-ons..." (same verb everywhere); "Sync new changes" and "Re-sync everything" (forgets what was synced and runs the whole sync again).
- Ratings: the "Date ratings with the watch date" option is gone; ratings are always dated with the watch.

v1.4.0
- Login: MDBList and Simkl now use the OAuth device flow (a PIN code on screen, approve on your phone). No API keys to type. Tokens renew by themselves. The status shows "Connected as @username". Client ids live only in services_config.py. MDBList users: log in again once (the API key is no longer used).
- Syncing: "From" and "To" lists (Kodi, MDBList, Simkl) replace the separate buttons. New pairs: Kodi -> MDBList, MDBList -> Kodi, Simkl -> MDBList. Preview, Sync now, Reset.
- Ratings: "Date ratings with the watch date" (on by default). A rating given before watching takes the watch date once you watch it; live ratings are dated with the watch.
- Fix: a stream that breaks mid-play (server timeout) no longer counts as finished: "ended" is trusted only when at most 2 minutes or 3 % were left, otherwise it is a normal stop at the real position (no watched mark, no rating prompt).
- Fix: Simkl title-only search never read Simkl's answer (a list), so it could not match; it does now.
- Setting renamed to "Ignore media shorter than (minutes)" (same value, now in the Ignore group).
- Under the hood: every service is a Provider (login, username, title search, history and ratings writes); adding one no longer touches the player.

v1.3.0
- New "General" tab: track only videos running at least X minutes (default 15; skips trailers and short clips), ignored folders (pick from your Kodi video sources, browse, or type a name) and ignored video add-ons (pick from the installed ones).
- Items with no ids and no year are now looked up by title only (MDBList search, or Simkl's search when MDBList is off). Ambiguous or unknown titles - family videos, clips - are ignored.
- New sync buttons between the Kodi library and Simkl, one per direction, each with a preview. Add-only. Simkl -> Kodi asks Simkl what changed first and reads only the changes.
- Fix: the MDBList -> Simkl sync now reads the watch date of plain item rows (`last_watched_at`) and files rated-but-never-watched items with their rating date instead of "today".
- Settings: six tabs on the left, connection status per account, shorter labels, one-line description. API keys are hidden.
- Removed: the extra service integration, the "match by title and year" option, Simkl title-only matching and the Simkl V1 login fallback.
- Fix: a Simkl token reply without a refresh token or scope no longer breaks the login.
